About BloodLink

BloodLink is a Smart Blood Donor and Request Management System designed to bridge the gap between blood donors, recipients, and hospitals through a single digital platform.

Our goal is to ensure that life‑saving blood reaches patients quickly and efficiently during emergencies. BloodLink simplifies communication and reduces delays by connecting people who are willing to donate blood with those who need it.

We aim to build a strong and responsible blood donation community by encouraging voluntary donation and spreading awareness about its importance.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a reliable and accessible system that helps save lives by ensuring blood availability whenever and wherever it is needed.

BloodLink focuses on improving healthcare coordination by providing fast donor search, emergency request management, and hospital blood inventory support.

Key Features

Donor Registration

Allows voluntary donors to register and update their availability easily.

Hospital Support

Hospitals can manage blood inventory and send emergency requests quickly.

Emergency Response

Recipients can find donors instantly during critical situations.

Our Vision

We envision a society where no life is lost due to blood shortage. BloodLink strives to create a nationwide donor network that promotes awareness, responsibility, and community support.
